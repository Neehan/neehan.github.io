## Research & Engineering

* **AutoFyn (2026)**: Autonomous agents that discovered 150+ vulnerabilities in major open-source projects. ([post](/blog/autofyn/), [code](https://github.com/SignalPilot-Labs/AutoFyn))
* **Discovery–Execution (2026)**: Predicts when longer reasoning improves model performance. ([post](/blog/discovery-execution/), [code](https://github.com/Neehan/DE-Framework))
* **VITA (2026)**: Transformer pretraining for climate-robust crop yield forecasting; AAAI oral presentation. ([post](/blog/vita/), [code](https://github.com/Neehan/VITA))
* **ML Augmented Binary Heap (2019)**: Uses machine learning to speed up binary heap operations. ([post](/blog/ml-augmented-heap/), [Medium](https://medium.com/@thankful_rose_ferret_864/how-we-sped-up-the-binary-heap-with-machine-learning-e10b3204e4e6))
